Monthly Giving

A small gift every month helps animals in need all year long.

Why give monthly

Ongoing support for animals in need

For over 25 years, the National Humane Society has worked to put an end to animal abuse and neglect. A monthly gift is support we can count on, month after month.

  • Set it once

    Choose an amount and your gift repeats every month, with nothing to remember.

  • Tax-deductible

    The National Humane Society is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so your gift is tax-deductible.

  • Help all year

    Steady gifts help fund spay/neuter, veterinary care and local shelters every month of the year.
