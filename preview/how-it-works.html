How the Sweepstakes Works

One drawing, one winner, and the winner chooses the prize. Here is how it works, start to finish.

1. Choose your ticket.

Sweepstakes tickets are $25.00. The winner has a choice of either any one car, or $50,000.00 cash.

With a Raffle Plus ticket the winner has their choice of any two cars, or one car and the cash prize.

2. Order online, by phone or by mail.

Tickets can be paid for by credit card, money order or check. Place an order online, call our order line at 844-807-4052, or send a check to 406 Suburban DR #364, Newark, DE 19711.

You can also buy a ticket as a gift: simply use the other person's name and contact information when completing your purchase.

3. Get your ticket numbers.

Your ticket numbers will be emailed or mailed to you within 48 hours of your purchase.

4. The drawing.

The winning ticket will be drawn on December 31, 2026. You do not need to be present to win. The winner will receive a phone call directly from us immediately following the drawing; if we cannot reach the winner by phone, we will email them.

5. Claiming the prize.

The winner must claim the prize within 30 days. Payment of all applicable federal, state and local taxes, fees and surcharges, tags and registration fees are the responsibility of the winner, who must provide proof of insurance to take delivery. If the cash prize is selected it will be paid in 3 installments, 60 days apart.

No purchase is necessary.

Please send a self-addressed stamped envelope to NHS, 406 Suburban DR #364, Newark, DE 19711.
