Start to finish

From your order to the drawing

  1. 1

    Order online, by phone or by mail

    Pay by credit card, money order or check. Order online, call our order line at 844-807-4052, or mail a check to 406 Suburban DR #364, Newark, DE 19711. To give a ticket as a gift, use the other person's name and contact details.

  2. 2

    Get your ticket numbers

    Your ticket numbers are emailed or mailed to you within 48 hours of your purchase.

  3. 3

    The drawing

    The winning ticket is drawn on December 31, 2026. You don't need to be present to win: we call the winner right after the drawing, and email them if we can't reach them by phone.

  4. 4

    Claim your prize

    The winner claims the prize within 30 days. Taxes, fees, tags and registration are the winner's responsibility, with proof of insurance to take delivery. A cash prize is paid in 3 installments, 60 days apart.

Good to know

No purchase is necessary

To enter without buying a ticket, send a self-addressed stamped envelope to NHS, 406 Suburban DR #364, Newark, DE 19711.
