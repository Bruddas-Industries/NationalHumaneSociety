How Sweepstakes Work

Sweepstakes-as-fundraising is a legitimate, regulated nonprofit model. Here's exactly how ours operates.

1. You enter.

Choose a prize, choose your entry tier (more entries = more chances to win). Or use the free Alternative Method of Entry by mail. Either way, you have an equal chance per entry.

2. We fund the mission.

Net proceeds — after the cost of the prize, payment processing, legal, and bonding fees — are granted to our shelter partners and used to fund the four mission pillars. The exact percentage is published on each prize page and in our audited financials.

3. We draw a winner.

Drawings are conducted by a licensed third-party administrator on the date listed on each prize page. Winners are contacted by phone and email within 7 days.

4. The winner takes the prize — or cash.

Every prize has a published cash alternative. Winners are responsible for taxes; we provide IRS Form W-2G when required.
