Frequently Asked Questions

How old do I have to be to purchase a Raffle ticket?

You must be 18 years or older to purchase a ticket for our sweepstakes.

How do I purchase a Raffle ticket?

Tickets are on sale on our website. Place an order online, send a check to 406 Suburban DR #364, Newark, DE 19711 or call our order line — 844 807 4052.

The winning ticket will be drawn on December 31, 2026.

Can I buy a ticket as a gift for or on behalf of someone else?

Yes! To purchase an entry for someone else, simply use their name and contact information when completing your purchase.

How can I get a copy of my ticket?

Your ticket numbers will be emailed or mailed to you within 48 hours of your purchase.

How will the winners be informed?

The lucky prize winner will receive a phone call directly from us immediately following the drawings. In the event that we cannot reach winners by phone, we will email them.

Do I have to be present for the drawing in order to win?

No, winners need not be present to win.

What is the value of the prizes?

Depending on the car that the winner chooses.

When are the drawings?

The winning ticket will be drawn on December 31, 2026.

What percentage of the money raised goes to charity?

100% of your donation goes to the National Humane Society, a 501(c)(3) organization.

All of the donations benefit animal shelters, Spay and Neuter programs to help reduce the large number of unwanted animals born in the US each year, Veterinarian bills for pet owners and other animal related aspects of our charitable mission.

A few of the groups that have benefited from donations are: Second Chance Animal Rescue Society, The Humane Society of the United States, American Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals, San Francisco Humane Society, Jacksonville Humane Society among many others.

How many tickets are sold?

Only 1000 participants will be allowed to purchase tickets.

Who provides the prizes?

Cars provided by Winner Automobile Group of Delaware and Porsche of Delaware.

If I still have questions about the National Humane Society's Charitable sweepstakes, who do I contact?

Send an e-mail to info@nationalhumanesociety.org or call us 877 285 0710 or 302 369 6532 and we will gladly clear up any questions you may have!
