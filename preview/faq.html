Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about entering the Sweepstakes and Raffle Plus.

Buying tickets

01How old do I have to be to purchase a Sweepstakes or Raffle Plus ticket?

You must be 18 years or older to purchase a ticket for our Sweepstakes or Raffle Plus.

02How do I purchase a Sweepstakes or Raffle Plus ticket?

Sweepstakes and Raffle Plus tickets are on sale on our website. Place an order online, send a check to 406 Suburban DR #364, Newark, DE 19711 or call our order line at 844-807-4052.

The winning ticket will be drawn on December 31, 2026.

03Can I buy a ticket as a gift for or on behalf of someone else?

Yes! To purchase an entry for someone else, simply use their name and contact information when completing your purchase.

04How can I get a copy of my ticket?

Your ticket numbers will be emailed or mailed to you within 48 hours of your purchase.

05Is there a limit on how many people can enter?

Yes. Only 1,000 participants can purchase tickets for each drawing.

The drawing

06When are the drawings?

The winning ticket will be drawn on December 31, 2026.

07Do I have to be present for the drawing in order to win?

No, winners do not need to be present to win.

08How will the winners be informed?

The lucky prize winner will receive a phone call directly from us immediately following the drawings. In the event that we cannot reach winners by phone, we will email them.

The prizes

09What is the value of the prizes?

Varies depending on the car or cash prize selected.

10Who provides the prizes?

Cars are provided by Winner Automobile Group of Delaware and Porsche of Delaware.

Where your money goes

11What percentage of the money raised goes to charity?

100% of your donation goes to the National Humane Society, a 501(c)(3) organization.

All of the donations benefit animal shelters, Spay and Neuter programs to help reduce the large number of unwanted animals born in the US each year, Veterinarian bills for pet owners and other animal related aspects of our charitable mission.

A few of the groups that have benefited from donations are: Second Chance Animal Rescue Society, The Humane Society of the United States, American Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals, San Francisco Humane Society, Jacksonville Humane Society among many others.

Still have questions?

12If I still have questions about the National Humane Society's Sweepstakes or Raffle Plus, who do I contact?

Send an e-mail to info@nationalhumanesociety.org or call us at 877-285-0710 and we will gladly clear up any questions you may have!

Still have questions?

We will gladly clear up any questions you may have.
