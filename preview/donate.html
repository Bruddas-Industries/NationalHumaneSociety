Direct, tax-deductible support for the four mission pillars. Choose monthly or one-time, choose an amount, that's it.

Why monthly giving matters most.

Predictable monthly support lets us commit to multi-year shelter partnerships, hire program staff, and respond to disasters within hours instead of weeks.

  • $10/month = [XX] meals for shelter animals annually
  • $25/month = one spay/neuter procedure every month
  • $50/month = a vet checkup for [X] animals every month
  • $100/month = a small operating grant to one partner shelter

All figures are placeholders pending real cost-per-outcome data.
